Why LLMs Outperform Legacy NMT

Instruction-Following Flexibility

Unlike NMT, LLMs follow general instructions rather than just performing translation. This allows them to adapt dynamically to specific domain requirements, brand voice, and style guides in real-time.

Unlimited Language Adaptability

From Canadian French to Swiss German, LLMs handle dialects with ease. There is no limit on domain specificity—"Patent Law Spanish" is just as valid a target as standard Spanish. No fine-tuning required.

Deeper Semantic Understanding

Trained on orders of magnitude more data, LLMs interpret meaning more deeply. They translate with idiomatic sensitivity and semantic depth that legacy pattern-matching NMT cannot match.

Context-Aware Precision

Our agents utilize the full context window—glossaries, previous segments, and surrounding document context—making fewer assumptions and significantly reducing fidelity errors.

Multi-modal Support

LLMs can digest images alongside text. Inclusion of technical illustrations directly within the context window ensures higher translation fidelity in complex technical documents.

Unlocking New Domains

The flexibility of LLMs enables high-quality machine translation in domains where NMT previously fell short, such as Gaming, Marketing, and Literature.
