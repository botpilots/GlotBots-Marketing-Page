I know a lot of people in localization are worried about how GenAI will affect their business. 

It’s not unfounded.

‣ Research confirms a second year in a row that an average LSP translation is not necessarily perceived as having higher quality than a generated one (see report WMT24 and especially WMT25)
‣ Google Translate recently started to use GenAI to achieve more idiomatic translations. This is comparable to when it started using NMT in 2016; a clear signal that a technological shift is due to happen again.

Nevertheless, It’s very important to stay grounded on what actually brings value to the customer - 𝗮 𝘀𝗲𝗻𝘀𝗲 𝗼𝗳 𝘂𝗻𝗾𝘂𝗲𝘀𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻𝗮𝗯𝗹𝗲 𝘁𝗿𝘂𝘀𝘁.

‣ No customer will ever trust a first draft more, no matter how well it was generated, than a final deliverable gone through certified QA by linguists who does the work with accountability and great pride. 
‣ Any product of reliable quality, needs to be tested in real-world conditions before being shipped. That's just how it works.

GenAI is very powerful, but it's just another step on the technological ladder the localization industry has been climbing since long. It will probably result in same efficiency gains as we got from NMT (2-3x gains). The industry has been through this before.

For the people who are worried, I advise you to act proactively and adopt the new tech. This will make you more knowledgeable and efficient, increase your margins, get more satisfied customers and if you're really on your toes, increase your revenue too. 

Just keep in mind that early adopter opportunity shrinks as time is passing, and that there's plenty of opportunity and little risk if you would choose to start the transition now.
