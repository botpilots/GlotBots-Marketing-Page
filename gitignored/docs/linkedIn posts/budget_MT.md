It's fascinating to see how little budget MT has in relation to other steps of an MTPE project. 

There's overhead, project management, TMS and last but not least, the post-editing and QA.

MT however, is a minuscule cost in comparison to the others. It's almost invincible if standard NMT MT translation services are being used, especially without any time put into creating a glossary.

But from a process quality perspective, this is completely upside down.

Any process which does not properly assure quality in its first steps will suffer considerably from it, as it's there where it matters the most. 

If this is not done, deficiencies produced early will continue downstreams resulting in expensive QA and post production, reduced and unreliable quality.

In the quest for greater quality in MT, LLM-based translations are particularly interesting because they by design allow for reflective iterative refinement of text, for example it can imitate the PDCA cycle. This is crucial in order to allow for a more high quality MT process.

In the future, we will probably see much higher budgets for the MT step, and the investment will pay off just as fast in lowered cost and increased quality.
