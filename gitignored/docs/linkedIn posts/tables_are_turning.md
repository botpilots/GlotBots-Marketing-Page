Tables are turning in the translation industry!

Smaller competitors can now start to compete with the giants, as LLMs are flattening the field. Now specialisation, not general inflexible solutions, is the most important ingredient to succeed.

DeepL may have models specialsed for translation, but they are not intelligent or used in an agentic way. Their models are limited to only input what's supposed to be translated, and to output nothing more than the target. 

In other words - they do not use a pipeline that reasons - reasons for example about what the most natural phrasing would be, how a nearby illustration affects the requirements or how a translation should be adapted to context and previous translations. Only a general purpose multi-modal LLM could do that.

BotPilots is a power user of AI, leveraging it to not only produce a translation, but also uses it to validate that the translation is good and aligned to the source segment, context and consistent with the customer's validated translations. It then allows the AI to refine it further, if it finds it necessary. 

Finally, we are model agnostic. We are a provider of translations, not models, and so we are able to rapidly adopt to any new model that would be more performant. When you choose BotPilots as a translation provider, you can be sure that for any language, the pipeline is state-of-the-art and quality is guaranteed.
