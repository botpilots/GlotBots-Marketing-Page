# The MTPE Efficiency Gap

Based on Nimdzi research, the "efficiency gap" refers to the discrepancy between the widespread adoption of Machine Translation Post-Editing (MTPE) and how effectively organizations actually leverage its full potential.

### What it means
While ~46% of projects now use MTPE (up from 26% in 2022), most organizations still treat MT output like traditional translation, employing full human review even when it's not needed. They fail to use the "missing links" of automation:
1. **Automatic Quality Estimation (AIQE)** – Knowing which segments actually need a human eye.
2. **Automatic Post-Editing (AutoPE)** – Using AI to fix recurring errors before a human ever sees them.

### Why it matters
Organizations that don't close this gap suffer from:
- **Over-editing:** Humans fixing things that are already "good enough" or could be fixed by AI.
- **Diminished Margins:** High costs for quality that could be achieved at a fraction of the price.
- **Speed Bottlenecks:** Manual steps slowing down a process that should be instantaneous.

### The GlotBots Solution
GlotBots is designed specifically to close this gap. By using **Agentic MT Systems** that reason, validate, and self-correct, we provide "AutoPE" by design. This reduces human post-editing effort by **2-3x**, finally realizing the cost and speed benefits that standard NMT providers leave on the table.
