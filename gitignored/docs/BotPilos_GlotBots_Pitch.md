## Technology Trends In Translation
LLMs are the next frontier in Machine Translation (and translation in general)

- LLMs are now leading MT quality in top benchmarks (WMT = the “Olympics” of MT).
- In fact, LLMs have outperformed legacy NMT 3 years in a row.
- Human translations are also being challenged by LLMs. In WMT25, the top LLM won in 14/15 language pairs, while human reference translations was only in top of 6/15.

**Definitions:**
- LLM = Large Language Model
- MT = Machine Translation
- NMT = Neural Machine Translation
- WMT = Workshop of Machine Translation

**Sources:** (Kocmi et al., WMT 2023), (Kocmi et al., WMT 2024), (Kocmi et al., WMT 2025)

## Market Trends in Translation
Growth in market size and MT usage

- Translation market is growing: Translation market is 70B USD and growing ~5% per year.
- MTPE adoption is increasing: among translation agencies (LSPs), the usage of the machine translation post editing (MTPE) process has been rapidly increasing, from 26% in 2022 to 45.8% in 2024.
- MT-only translations are popular: LSP Smartling confirms MT-only deliverables represents as much as 43% of their total volume.

## LLM vs NMT
Why are LLMs better than legacy NMT?

- LLMs can adapt language to domain.
- LLMs can adapt in real-time to brand language.
- LLMs supports dialects (e.g. Canadian French, Swiss German).
- LLMs require no fine-tuning to be SOTA. Onboarding is instant.
- LLMs supports iterative refinement workflows.
- LLMs supports idiomatic translations (enabling new domain use such as Gaming, Marketing).
- LLMs support multi-modal context (e.g. illustrations).

## Output Quality depends on the Input Quality
Agentic MT Systems (not only LLMs!) are needed to realise cost benefits

- If LLM translations are not orchestrated properly, the overall cost reduction becomes marginal at best.
- It's first when LLMs gets the right context that they become next generation translation machines.
- Examples are glossaries, similar sentences, surrounding context, character descriptions, style-guides, illustrations etc.
- This is why domain specialisation of the system is of such great importance when using LLM models for translation.

The Giants of Machine Translation Are in Deep Mud
